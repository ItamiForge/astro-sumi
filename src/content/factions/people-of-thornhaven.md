# People of Thornhaven

Ordinary people in an ordinary valley, which is the point. The epic does not happen to heroes in a vacuum. It happens to a bronze-smith named Kael, a boy with a wooden sword, a woman grinding grain, and elders who would rather not believe.

They are what the Five stand in front of.
