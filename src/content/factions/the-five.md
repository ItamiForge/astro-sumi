# The Five

The prophecy is older than bronze: when darkness threatens to consume the world, five heroes rise. They have risen before. They will rise again.

In the Age of Bronze they are:

* [[character:kael|Kael]], the Warrior
* [[character:mira|Mira]], the Mage
* [[character:lyssa|Lyssa]], the Healer
* [[character:ryn|Ryn]], the Shadow
* [[character:aria|Aria]], the Seer

The Bond between them is not a metaphor. In battle they move as one body. How the Bond is forged, and whether the cycle can ever be broken, is the long work of the volumes that follow.
