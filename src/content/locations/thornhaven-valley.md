# Thornhaven Valley

The valley sits between two rivers, protected by ancient oak forests and blessed with soil that has fed the same families longer than anyone bothers to count.

[[location:thornhaven|Thornhaven]] village occupies the heart of it. North, the oaks thicken into the [[location:northern-forest|northern forest]], and beyond that a pass the hunters have started to fear.

It is a good place. A safe place. Until recently.
