# Northern Forest

The northern forest has always been the village's hunting ground and its wall. Wolves, deer, oak, and a path toward the [[location:northern-pass|northern pass]].

Something in it has changed. Three hunters went in. One came back, raving about darkness that swallows light. [[character:elder-thom|Elder Thom]] calls it fever. [[character:lyssa|Lyssa]] does not.

The forest is not the Darkness. It is the first room the Darkness has entered.
