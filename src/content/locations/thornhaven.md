# Thornhaven

Thornhaven is the heart of its valley: huts, a bronze-smith, a healer's door, fields, and a hunter who sleeps badly.

[[character:kael|Kael]] lives at the edge. [[character:lyssa|Lyssa]] walks the lanes with herbs and a gift she tries to hide. [[character:kael-the-elder|Kael the Elder]] hammers plow blades. [[character:elder-thom|Elder Thom]] would like the morning to remain ordinary.

Ordinary is ending. People glance toward the northern forest the way you glance at a storm.

## Places within reach

* The goat pens and grain-stone
* The smith's open shed
* The oak line Kael walks at dawn
* The road that becomes the northern path
