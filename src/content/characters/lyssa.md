# Lyssa

Lyssa keeps Thornhaven standing in the small ways: fever, birth, a hunter's torn shoulder. The elders distrust anything that looks like magic, so she hides how far her hands can go.

She has been dreaming other lives too. When she tells [[character:kael|Kael]] about the hunter who came back raving from the northern forest, she already knows the Darkness is not a fever.

Among the Five she is the heart: the Healer who binds the others when bronze and will are not enough.
