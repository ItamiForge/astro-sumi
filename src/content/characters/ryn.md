# Ryn

Ryn is the Shadow of the Five: master of stealth and the unasked question. His methods make the village uneasy. His loyalty does not.

In the northern pass he moves like a second thought beside [[character:kael|Kael]], daggers finding the places bronze swords are too honest to look for.

He has always walked between light and dark. This age is no different. The Darkness, however, is.
