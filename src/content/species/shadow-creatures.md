# Shadow-creatures

They were animals. The Darkness does not make armies from nothing in this age; it ruins what the forest already has.

Wolves with eyes like burning coals. Bears whose fur drips black ichor. Birds whose wings blot out the sun. White fire unmakes them. Bronze can, if it is swung by someone whose hands remember war.

They are the first wave, not the war.
