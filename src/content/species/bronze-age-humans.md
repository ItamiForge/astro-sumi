# Humans of the Bronze Age

The people of Thornhaven are human in the oldest sense: finite, local, stubborn. Their tools are bronze. Their calendar is seasons. Their magic, when it appears, is treated as a problem.

Some of them are also older than that. The Five wear human lives the way a sword wears a scabbard. The scabbard still bleeds.
